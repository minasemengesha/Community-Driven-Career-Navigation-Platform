How can we help?

Find answers or contact the RightGuide support team about your account, career resources, promotions, or payments.

What do you need help with?

  • Account access: Sign-in, registration, or account details.
  • Career resources: Questions or issues with learning and career content.
  • Promotions and payments: Promotion access, charges, or payment questions.

Frequently asked questions

I cannot sign in to my account. What should I do?

Check that you entered the email address and password associated with your account. If you still cannot sign in, contact support and describe the issue. Never send your password.

How do I report a problem with a career resource?

Contact support with the resource name, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Do not include private account or payment details.

Where can I ask about a promotion or payment?

Email support with a brief description of the promotion or payment issue. For your safety, do not send card numbers, security codes, passwords, or other sensitive financial information.

What information should I include in a support request?

Include the email address on your account, the page or feature involved, and a clear description of the issue. If you attach a screenshot, remove passwords and sensitive personal or financial information first.
