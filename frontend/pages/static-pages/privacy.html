Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Community-Driven Career Navigation Platform. We respect the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when people use our platform and how that information is used.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the platform may collect:

  • Account information provided during registration.
  • Learning resource information submitted by users.
  • Voting and recommendation activity.
  • Promotion request and payment verification information.
  • Information needed to respond to customer support requests.

3. Guest User Information

Visitors can browse and recommend learning resources without creating an account. When a guest submits a recommendation, the platform is designed to assign a unique guest identifier. This helps identify repeated recommendations for the same resource from the same guest identifier.

The guest identifier does not verify a person's real-world identity.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Manage user accounts.
  • Display and organize learning resources.
  • Review and manage recommendations.
  • Help identify duplicate recommendations.
  • Manage promotions and verify submitted payment evidence.
  • Provide customer support and improve the platform.

5. Payment Information

Users requesting paid promotions may be required to submit payment evidence. This information is used for payment verification.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. User information may be accessed by authorized platform administrators and support agents when necessary to manage accounts, recommendations, promotions, and customer support.

7. Data Security

The platform aims to protect user information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse through appropriate security measures.

8. User Privacy Requests

Users who have questions or concerns about their personal information can contact the platform's support team.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the platform's features or information-handling practices change. Any updated policy will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our customer support team.
