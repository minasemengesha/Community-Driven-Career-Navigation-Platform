Welcome to Right Guidance!

We believe finding quality learning resources should be simple and accessible. Right Guidance brings educational websites and YouTube channels together in one organized place, helping learners discover useful resources and explore new subjects.

These Terms and Conditions explain how the platform works and what we expect from everyone who uses it.

01

Introduction

Right Guidance is an educational resource discovery and recommendation platform designed to make learning easier. It helps visitors find educational websites, YouTube channels, and other useful learning materials organized by subject and category.

Users can explore the Resource Directory, suggest new Learning Topics, and recommend resources that may benefit other learners. Registered Creators can also contribute through their accounts and become eligible for Contributor Points when their recommendations are accepted.

By accessing or using Right Guidance, you agree to follow these Terms and Conditions. Please take a moment to read them before using the platform.

02

Acceptance of Terms

When you visit Right Guidance, browse its resources, create an account, or submit a recommendation, you agree to use the platform according to these terms.

These rules apply to all users, whether you are simply exploring learning resources or actively contributing to the platform.

If you do not agree with these conditions, please discontinue using the platform. Some services may also have additional instructions that must be followed when using those services.

03

User Accounts and Roles

Right Guidance provides different ways to participate. Some activities are available to everyone, while others require a registered account or authorized platform access.

General Visitors

You do not need to register or log in just to explore the Resource Directory. General Visitors can browse learning topics, discover educational resources, and submit general recommendations without creating an account.

Registered Creators

Registered Creators can access their accounts and submit Creator Recommendations through the platform. They may also qualify for Contributor Points when their recommendations are reviewed and accepted as valid by an Administrator.

Administrators

Administrators help maintain the quality of Right Guidance. Their responsibilities include reviewing submitted resources, applying evaluation criteria, approving or rejecting recommendations, assigning resource rankings, and allocating Contributor Points.

Customer Support Agents

Authorized Customer Support Agents help users with payment-related questions, manually verify submitted Payment Evidence, communicate payment results, and handle complaints about promotional services.

Your Account Responsibilities

If you create an account, you are responsible for providing accurate information and protecting your login details. Do not share your password or allow others to use your account without permission.

If you notice suspicious activity on your account, contact the platform's support team.

04

Learning Resources and the Resource Directory

The Resource Directory is one of the main features of Right Guidance. It brings together educational resources so learners can find useful materials without having to search through everything separately.

Resources may include educational YouTube channels, learning websites, and other online materials related to different subjects, such as programming, technology, design, and more.

Each resource may be organized under a Learning Topic and Resource Category to make browsing easier. We aim to provide a helpful collection, but we cannot guarantee that every external resource will always be available, accurate, or suitable for every learner.

Some resources are owned and managed by third parties. Their content, services, and policies are controlled by their respective owners, not by Right Guidance. Please review the relevant third-party terms when visiting external websites or platforms.

05

Topic and Resource Recommendations

A good learning resource can help someone discover a new skill or understand a difficult subject. That is why Right Guidance allows users to suggest topics and recommend useful educational resources.

Topic Requests

If you cannot find a subject you are interested in, you may submit a Topic Request. This helps us understand what learners want to explore next. However, submitting a request does not guarantee that the topic will be added.

General User Recommendations

General Visitors can recommend educational websites or YouTube channels without registering. These suggestions give everyone an opportunity to help improve the Resource Directory.

Creator Recommendations

Registered Creators can submit recommendations through their authenticated accounts. These contributions may qualify for Contributor Points after an Administrator reviews and accepts them.

What Makes a Good Recommendation?

To help other learners, recommendations should contain genuine and relevant educational resources. Please make sure that the information and links you provide are accurate.

  • The resource should match the selected Learning Topic.
  • The link should lead to the correct website or channel.
  • The resource should provide meaningful educational value.
  • The submission should not contain harmful or misleading content.
  • You should not submit repeated recommendations to manipulate counts or rewards.

Duplicate Recommendations

Sometimes different users may recommend the same educational resource. Rather than filling the directory with repeated entries, Right Guidance may group recommendations for the same resource. This process is called Recommendation Aggregation.

The platform may retain the total Recommendation Count while displaying the resource as a single organized entry.

06

Resource Evaluation and Approval

Not every submitted resource is automatically published. To help maintain a useful and trustworthy directory, submitted recommendations may be manually examined by an Administrator.

How Resources Are Evaluated

Administrators may consider several factors, including educational relevance, content quality, usefulness, accessibility, working links, and suitability for the intended Learning Topic.

Recommendation Status

After submission, a recommendation may receive one of the following statuses:

  • Awaiting Review: The recommendation has been submitted and is waiting for an Administrator to examine it.
  • Accepted: The resource has met the platform's evaluation requirements and has been approved.
  • Rejected: The resource has not met the required standards and will not be approved for inclusion.

Resource Ranking

Administrators may assign rankings to resources based on their evaluation. Recommendation Count may be considered, but a high number of recommendations does not automatically mean that a resource will receive a high ranking.

Right Guidance may update rankings, review previously accepted resources, or remove resources when necessary to maintain directory quality.

07

Contributor Points and Rewards

We appreciate users who take the time to share valuable educational resources. To recognize meaningful contributions, Right Guidance may award Contributor Points to eligible Registered Creators.

How Points Are Earned

Points are awarded only when a recommendation submitted by a Registered Creator is reviewed and accepted as valid by an Administrator. Simply submitting a recommendation does not guarantee points.

Rejected, invalid, misleading, or intentionally duplicated submissions are not eligible for Contributor Points.

Contributor Rewards

Creators who meet the platform's reward requirements may become eligible for benefits such as a Free Promotion Reward. This may allow an eligible resource to receive promotional visibility without the normal promotion fee, subject to the applicable reward conditions.

Contributor Points are participation points, not money. They cannot automatically be exchanged for cash and do not have a guaranteed monetary value.

If points are awarded incorrectly or obtained through dishonest activity, Right Guidance may correct the account balance or withdraw the incorrectly awarded points.

08

Paid Promotion Services

Right Guidance may offer promotional opportunities for eligible educational websites and YouTube channels. These services are designed to give approved resources additional visibility within the platform.

Submitting a Promotion Request

A resource owner or authorized representative may submit a Promotion Request through the available promotion process. The submitted resource must still meet the platform's eligibility and evaluation requirements.

Promotion Fees and Placement

Promotion Fees depend on the available promotional options. The applicable fee and placement conditions should be communicated before the user proceeds with payment.

Promotion Placement may vary depending on the selected option and amount paid. However, promotional placement does not guarantee a particular number of views, clicks, visitors, subscribers, or learning outcomes.

Approval of Promoted Resources

Paying for promotion does not automatically mean that a resource will be approved. Right Guidance may reject resources that are misleading, harmful, unsuitable, or inconsistent with the platform's educational purpose.

Promotional visibility should not be understood as a guarantee that every statement or service offered by the promoted resource has been independently verified by Right Guidance.

09

Payments and Payment Verification

Some promotional services require payment. To help confirm these transactions, users may be asked to submit Payment Evidence through the designated Payment Page.

Submitting Payment Evidence

Payment Evidence may include a screenshot showing the relevant payment transaction. The uploaded evidence should be genuine, readable, and related to the Promotion Request.

Please check your submission before uploading it. Do not provide false screenshots or information that could mislead the support team.

Manual Payment Verification

Unlike an automatic payment confirmation system, Right Guidance uses manual verification by an authorized Customer Support Agent. The agent reviews the submitted evidence to determine whether the payment can be confirmed.

This means uploading a screenshot alone does not confirm that your payment was successful. The payment must first be checked and its status communicated through the platform's verification process.

Payment Status

After verification, a payment may be recorded as one of the following:

  • Confirmed Successful: The submitted payment has been verified.
  • Unsuccessful: The payment could not be verified or did not meet the required conditions.

Payment Complaints and Refund Requests

If you believe that your payment has been incorrectly recorded or your promotion request has not been handled properly, you may contact Customer Support for assistance.

Refunds are not automatic or guaranteed. Each refund request should be reviewed according to the circumstances, applicable law, and the platform's actual payment arrangements.

A promotion should proceed only after the necessary payment verification and resource approval have been completed.

These items help highlight selected resources and communicate useful information to visitors. They may appear in homepage sections, promotional areas, or popup messages.

A resource may be featured because it has been selected by an Administrator or has received an approved promotional placement.

Right Guidance may update, rearrange, or remove advertisements and featured content when needed. The appearance of a resource on the platform should not be treated as a guarantee of its quality or of every claim made by its owner.

User Responsibilities and Prohibited Activities

A useful learning platform depends on honest contributions and respectful participation. Everyone using Right Guidance is expected to act responsibly and avoid activities that could harm other users or the platform.

Activities That Are Not Allowed

  • Submitting fake, misleading, or intentionally harmful educational recommendations.
  • Repeating recommendations to unfairly increase Recommendation Counts or earn additional points.
  • Uploading false payment screenshots or providing dishonest transaction information.
  • Attempting to access another person's account or restricted platform features without permission.
  • Sharing malicious links, malware, or content intended to harm users.
  • Impersonating another individual, organization, or resource owner.
  • Using the platform for fraudulent, abusive, or unlawful activities.
  • Interfering with the platform's normal operation, availability, or security.

If a user violates these rules, Right Guidance may reject their submissions, remove unsuitable content, correct improperly awarded points, or restrict access to the relevant account.
