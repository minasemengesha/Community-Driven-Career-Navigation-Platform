These terms explain the rules and responsibilities that apply when using our platform.

2. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using Right Guidance, you agree to follow these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree, you should stop using the website.

3. User Roles

Our platform supports the following user roles:

General Visitor
Can explore resources and submit general recommendations.
Registered Creator
Can submit creator recommendations and may earn contributor points.
Administrator
Reviews resources, manages content, and awards points.
Customer Support Agent
Verifies payments and handles promotion complaints.

4. Learning Resources

Our Resource Directory organizes educational websites and YouTube channels by learning topics such as HTML, CSS, programming, and technology.

External resources belong to their respective owners. Users should review third-party content before relying on it.

5. Topic and Resource Recommendations

Visitors may suggest new learning topics and educational resources without creating an account. Registered Creators may submit recommendations through their accounts.

Recommendations should be relevant, useful, accurate, and appropriate for educational purposes.

6. Resource Evaluation and Approval

Administrators manually examine submitted resources using criteria such as educational quality, relevance, usefulness, and reliability.

Each recommendation may have one of these statuses:

  • Awaiting Review
  • Accepted
  • Rejected

7. Contributor Points and Rewards

Registered Creators may receive contributor points when administrators accept their valid recommendations. Submitting a recommendation does not guarantee points.

Eligible contributors may qualify for rewards, including free promotion, according to platform rules.

8. Paid Promotion Services

Users may request paid promotion for eligible educational resources. Promotion requests must satisfy the platform's requirements.

Payment alone does not guarantee approval or placement of a promoted resource.

9. Payments and Verification

Users submitting promotion requests must provide the required payment evidence through the payment page. A Customer Support Agent manually checks the evidence.

Payment verification can result in two statuses:

  • Confirmed Successful
  • Unsuccessful

Uploading a payment screenshot does not automatically mean that the payment has been confirmed.

10. User Responsibilities

All users are expected to:

  • Provide honest and accurate information.
  • Submit appropriate educational resources.
  • Respect other people's intellectual property.
  • Avoid harmful, misleading, or inappropriate content.
  • Use the platform responsibly and fairly.

11. Privacy and Account Security

Users should protect their account credentials and avoid sharing confidential information. Personal information and payment evidence should be handled according to the Privacy Policy.
